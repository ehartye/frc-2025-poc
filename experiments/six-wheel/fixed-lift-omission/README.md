## What the scale must establish

The selected purchasing crosswalk identifies three REV-21-1650 motors. Two drive rows each carry the manufacturer's nominal 425 g. The receiver lift explicitly selects the third article. Its mass is embedded in a 1.8–3.9 kg group containing the drive, ratchet, lines, drums and hardware. The original ledger gives **no minimum allocated specifically to that motor**. Removing its CAD occurrence establishes the count of two. It cannot convert the group minimum into a guaranteed 425 g credit. The historical release did not remove this unit when the air circuit was retired. ## Fit and assemble the stationary closure

Use **only the original am-3770 full-shaft block** in this study. Its supplier solid has a flat motor side, Ø19.05 centre bore and two motor-screw through holes on the 50.80 mm diagonal. A different pair retains the gearbox stack. The short-block configuration shares long through screws with the motor and needs its own closure. The sourced input is a motor pinion pressed onto the shaft. Confirm which motor, pinion, ratchet and independent housing mount the historical robot actually has before selecting the low fixed build. A gear supported by the missing motor shaft, retention that relies on motor bearing preload or a different mounting block fails this design. [2017 supplier drawing](https://documents.andymark.com/4024585a74c02cfe69c3bc07df957d05170cc1efd4970d63063e98530a7be620/am-3770%20-%20Cim%20Sport%20Standard%20Motor%20Block.PDF) · [Block variants](https://andymark.com/products/sport-gearbox-motor-blocks?sku=am-3765) · [Assembly guide](https://s3.amazonaws.com/docusync-files/d5679fbc5bb914b59bd5ada5919979679c0be3ba422db306c3745d20358da398/am-3972%20CIM%20Sport%20Motor%20Installation.pdf)

1. Support and unload the carriage in a rated external fixture, lift energy isolated. Verify both fixed-height shoulder pins, sleeves, two washers per side and locking nuts. Record the actual configuration and support path before servicing the transmission. The fixed configuration offers no powered height adjustment or midmatch homing.
2. Assemble the block and closure on the bench. Retain and register the gearbox thrust washer and two separate case screws. Keep the motor, its input pinion and any shaft hardware out of the finished gearbox. Check the internal stages remain captured in every orientation; the external cap only covers the opening.
3. Machine the cap from verified aluminum stock to the [drawing](shop.svg). Its nominal net volume is 9,743.21 mm³. Model threads at their major envelope. Gauge both real #10-32 tapped holes and inspect for breakout and burrs. Machine the flat face without a protruding pilot. A 0.10 mm counterfactual press into the supplied block produces 163.20 mm³ of common material, while the correctly seated cap begins at zero gap and zero volume overlap.
4. Fit the two proposed #10-32 × 1.000 in socket screws through the inner counterbores before reassembling the case. Actual head dimensions must fit the modeled Ø7.8 × 4.7 mm envelope. At the nominal 18.288 mm shoulder depth the cap receives 7.112 mm of screw, with 1.888 mm remaining to the back. A 1.250 in substitution projects 4.462 mm beyond the bosses. Measure lead and effective engagement (target at least 6.0 mm of full joint thread), counterbore, cap flatness, thread fit, retention and witness marks. Preserve the manufacturer stack arrangement. A short-drop screw, a loose stage, inaccessible retention or an unsupported housing rejects the assembly.
5. Preserve both drive controllers, all other motor branches and CAN continuity. The earlier project allocated PDH 2, CAN 13 exclusively to lift. Keep the controller, branch budget and associated hardware in the mass record. Require de-energized isolated and secured phase/encoder ends, tagged branch identification and independent neutral-on-boot/fault behavior. Verify installed CAN termination and the expected missing lift encoder diagnostics on the bench. The actual firmware and cables are uncommissioned.

Native motion checking covers the selected source robot, moving-stage/pinion allowances, additional optical heads and routed wire reserves. The cap stays at least 8.418 mm from another registered robot group and 90.891 mm from the added camera/wire groups. Two screw envelopes clear the actual vendor block. Both cap and motor are hidden at the six nominal rendered endpoints in both eyes. The changed geometry requires new provenance for a complete moving-stroke camera claim. The fixed harness and guide options entered the combined **weight** comparison; their new physical attachments and a positive cap installation still need inspection. ## Permanent supports and fault response

At the current load both sides independently give positive native crossbar-to-pin, pin-to-sleeve and sleeve-to-mast contact after 1 mm downward displacement. Use the fixed-height baseline plus the entire additional 440 g camera allocation and 1.376792 kg moving upper increment as a conservative lift load. This yields **14.298 kg** on one side. At 3 g the single-pin load is 420.64 N, nominal cantilever bending 150.63 MPa and average two-wall bearing 8.76 MPa. The 10 g control reaches 502.10 MPa. Check actual material, standoff, dynamic peak, assembly preload, threads outside the shear planes, local bearing, crossbar welds, pin capture and deck/frame restraint. Positive-volume counterfactuals establish a mechanical path; they do not qualify its load. The passive ratchet and rope remain separately unqualified redundancy. One missing pin or keeper, a disturbed lock, failed shelf witness or unknown height requires inhibited movement and externally supported service. Do not substitute brake torque from the omitted motor.

The actual 35 released desktop cases contain 18 HOLD events. The observed command sequence operates drive, grip and slide with a fixed carriage. Every predecessor shared-bus case records zero lift phase and branch current. Removing a fixed motor does not reduce the modeled moving mass, remove the slide, certify drive traction or change the modeled rack acknowledgement deadline. The older variable-height StationCycle demands a lift and is incompatible with this build. Renew physical input/output, fastening, support and full match qualification before use. ## Why the deck stays intact

The 692 × 516 × 4 mm deck bears on the front and rear 20 mm tubes and on the edges of the two inner drive rails. The selected assembly supplies no positive deck-to-frame connector reacting uplift. Battery and restraint loads enter through the aft tray. Electronics use a separate rear backplate and four front platform posts. The mast feet sit near the inner rail edges. The existing harness anchor bears on the deck near Y195, while the moving cable's dedicated supports and its newer fixed-harness foot use their respective mounts. Preserve the lamp platform extension, observer foot, and backplate when planning a new deck joint. A front shelf acting 200 mm beyond the assumed support would add about 124 N to the end-rail couple at 3 g. An illustrative horizontal 1 kN load 350 mm above the deck increases that couple by 515 N. Plain face contact cannot carry tension under reversal.

The proposed six underside pockets remove 82,364.6 mm³ or 222.384 g nominal. At their full section they leave 78.43% of the longitudinal second moment and 17.80% of the original local plate rigidity. A simple-supported strip model at 68.3 GPa spans 464 mm across the two inner rails, neglecting longitudinal load sharing and beneficial end supports. The 6.17 kg battery distributed across its 76 mm X footprint at 3 g gives 13.54 mm nominal deflection and 103.39 MPa in the uncut aft strip. The controller/wire strip (0.4 kg at 3 g) rises from 0.54 to 1.68 mm. At a 100 N service load borne over a 30 mm strip on a thinned patch the calculation changes from 18.60 to 68.05 mm and 143.18 to 452.51 MPa. Large predicted deflections exceed small-deflection assumptions and trigger rejection. They are not predictions of permanent set. No plate removal or reclaimed metal enters the selected robot. Check actual deck uplift, battery restraints, deflection and local electronics service loads in a separately supported first article.
