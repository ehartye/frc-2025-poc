The installed 692 × 516 × 4 mm deck bears on two end tubes and both inner side rails. Previously it could lift away from all four supports. The proposed joint carries uplift from the plate through a steel washer and nut into a one-piece aluminum tie whose foot hooks both walls of each welded end tube. Two flats on an inboard lip stop the foot turning. The existing weld preparations and bumper screws survive. The receiver, battery tray and electronics still need their own attachment proofs. ## Drill and fit the chassis

1. Locate both end rails and the deck from the chassis center plane and crossrail faces. With +X forward and +Y left, the three rear axes are (−339,−225), (−339,+72), (−339,+225) mm. Front axes are (+339,−225), (+339,−72), (+339,+225). Use the square unpocketed sheet. Register the three machined parts by their original IDs `deck-main`, `cross-front`, and `cross-rear`; the exchange prefixes those names with `tie-machined-`.
2. On the bare weldment, use a removable two-face fixture. Match-drill and deburr six Ø5.50 (+0.10/0) bores through the sheet and **upper** tube wall. Drill Ø8.20 (+0.08/0) through the **lower** wall at those axes. Limit position to ±0.10 mm from the surveyed rail datums and **0.10 mm total coaxial offset** between the lower bore and upper pair. Prove the finished pair with the actual tie. Protect the far wall and remove all swarf from the closed section. Keep a 15 mm wide solid strip from the end of the deck locally around each station. The outside deck edge is 7 mm from the stud center.
3. Make six ties from certified 7075-T7351 bar, minimum project purchase requirement 400 MPa tensile yield. Use a blank at least 32 mm in diameter. Finish the Ø8 stem to 7.95–8.00 mm and the plain pilot above the shoulder to 4.90–4.98 mm. Cut M5×0.8-6g on the upper end, with full thread through the nut and at least two pitches past the locking insert. The simplified exchange shows the thread's major envelope. Chamfer the thread entry, gauge with GO/NO-GO rings, finish the transition without a sharp tool groove, protect the alloy surfaces against fretting/galvanic attack and verify the **finished** stem/lip fit. Inspect the thread root and shoulder on the first part.
4. The foot is 19.5 × 22 × 4 mm plus its inboard extension. Its main rectangle centers on |X|340, 1 mm outboard of the stud. The key runs the full 22 mm along Y, extends inward to |X|328 and rises 3 mm above the rail’s bottom face, ending at |X|329.8. Hold 0.10–0.30 mm to the actual inboard web. The foot requires two flat bearing strips at least 1.75 mm wide directly below the tube webs. Fit each stem shoulder to the **measured** bottom outer face → upper inner face distance minus 0–0.10 mm (97.95 nominal). A 0.50 mm overlength neck seats against the upper wall before the foot can seat. Blue both web contact strips, verify full contact with a feeler, and stamp matched station marks.
5. Bore Ø5.40 (+0.05/0) from the lower face, depth 90 mm **maximum to the drill tip**. Require ≥1.20 mm minimum remaining wall anywhere after runout. Leave solid metal at the upper transition. A supported turning setup and guided long drill are necessary. Check the initial part's wall distribution, runout and shoulder radii; substitute solid stock only after recomputing mass.
6. Seat the foot register, fit one BLOHM B0112.05 5.3/10 × 1 mm steel washer and one Würth 03715 DIN 982 class 8 high-pattern locknut (8 AF, 6.3 mm high) per tie. Measure **at least 4 mm of load-bearing metal thread** separately from the insert. Nominal exposed thread is 2.7 mm, 3.4 pitches. Charge six each of anchors, washers and nuts. Replacement locknuts and the holding fork are shop stock outside the installed kit. [Nut](https://eshop.wuerth.de/Hexagon-nut-high-profile-with-clamping-piece-non-metal-insert-DIN-982-steel-8-zinc-plated-blue-passivated-A2K-NUT-HEX-SLOK-DIN982-8-WS8-A2K-M5/03715.sku/en/US/EUR/) · [Washer](https://www.blohm-gmbh.de/de/en/Products/Product-overview/Machine-and-fixture-components/Screws-nuts-and-washers/Washersmedium-DIN-EN-ISO-7089-A.html) · [Kaiser stock data](https://online.kaiseraluminum.com/depot/PublicProductInformation/Document/1028/Kaiser_Aluminum_7075_Rod_and_Bar.pdf)

**Service on a de-energized, independently supported chassis.** Leave at least 130 mm unobstructed beneath each end rail. Keep the other five ties in place to replace one. Hold the bottom foot with a 16 mm wide fork across its Y flats (22.3 mm jaw opening, 3.8 mm engagement) while seating or releasing the nut. The fixture approaches from below. Bring a compact 8 AF deep socket, ≤13 mm OD and ≤21.8 mm long, horizontally over the bumper with its bottom above Z197. Lower it onto the nut. Its compact outward-pointing drive must clear the 45 mm handle envelope shown by the native check; use a slim extension to put the underside of the drive 40 mm above the socket mouth. Lift the nut and washer, then lower the integral tie 124 mm. Check real tooling, stand, hand protection, wire dressing and off-robot access before releasing a shop procedure. Deck removal additionally needs all equipment whose feet or wiring cross the deck boundary disconnected and independently supported.

## What the model can establish

The source contains 991 optical/receiver objects. The fit run adds the welded crossrail machining, current chain-cover supports and front-chain repack, the fixed camera's real harness fittings and conservative jacket volumes, the optional narrow guide, and the retained motor/cover variants. It bounds 303 moving bodies through their endpoint envelopes and uses the source’s rotating-part bounds. New material is checked at all six stations, and tool and withdrawal gauges sweep their full paths. Each 0.2 mm axial press contacts the intended upstream member; both signs of lip rotation meet the web. The geometry at Y+175 would put the through-tie in the original bumper screw and sleeve. At rear Y−72 the trial nut cuts 56.45 mm³ of the camera cable raceway. A revised six-station steel-through-bolt package cleared these obstacles; its 24 added parts exceed the available 123.070 g even with the surrendered motor. [Steel alternative](steel-control.json) · [Source and load calculations](load.py)

The load screen places the full 67 kg at X/Y offsets to ±100 mm and Z500, applies ±3 g vertically plus 1 kN horizontally at every heading, and distributes the force and moment over six equal-stiffness ties. The whole machine weighs more than the equipment borne directly by the deck. The real plate/web stiffness and equipment attachment distribution can concentrate individual reactions. The former isolated battery-strip screen reached 13.54 mm under 3 g; none of the new ties adds a central battery support. [Original plate screening](../fixed-lift-omission/mechanics.json)

The nominal six-tie case reaches about **1.14 kN tension and 230 N shear** on the worst tie. The model bounds total thread demand by adding all external tension to an unmeasured 850 N preload. The net Ø3.95 root gives 166 MPa equivalent nominal stress, or 265 MPa at an assumed 1.6 local factor. The 4 mm foot spanning 16 mm between webs gives 180 MPa and 0.027 mm calculated deflection. Losing one tie raises estimated demand to **1.52 kN tension**, 319 MPa at the same factor and 215 MPa at the foot. Adopt no one-missing service permission. The blind-bored stem is tensile under uplift. The upper shoulder can bypass part of the clamping load; the bound allows up to 0.10 mm initial take-up plus elastic elongation (0.178 mm total in the six-tie case). Local hole pull-through, wall dimpling, actual thread load shares, cut-root concentration, stress corrosion, fatigue, overturning of a loose deck and joint settling all need physical evidence. [Load receipt](load.json)

The proposed shelf-contact study places about 3.39 kN **downward** for about 2.2 ms near X116, Y±2, Z499. Its resultant crosses the deck inside the support polygon. Both ideal global distributions are compressive at every tie. The downward impulse is about 2.59 N·s; these data cannot qualify the upstream nested rails, mast feet, local deck bending or rebound. In particular, a rigid-body screening cannot convert 2 ms contact into an allowable static load on the deck.

## Issue and inspection

**Check nut installation torque before choosing the final production lot.** At 850 N trial preload, a conservative model sending the full wrench torsion through the Ø3.95 root reaches the 400 MPa project minimum at **1.678 N·m gross**, including insert drag. A 2.00 N·m installation gives **471 MPa** at the trial local factor. Both the torque and preload on the actual finished aluminum thread must be measured; the calculated threshold is a screening ceiling. Reject a nut/finish combination that needs more torque to reach the intended preload. A visible keyed foot cannot protect the threaded tip from overtorque.

Weigh the full coated kit and inspection robot. On a duplicate actual rail section, use a load cell to characterize the locknut's running torque and choose an installation process for 850 N trial preload; measure before and after settling. Under controlled lab loading, increase tensile demand through the real deck/washer/stud/web joint to the conservative 1.99 kN six-tie combination, and shear separately to at least the modeled demand. Record seating displacement, rebound, any permanent dish or thread damage, creep/settling and retained nut position after repeated representative loading. Investigate prying/loading direction and establish the justified proof margin and cycle count with the responsible engineer. This design has no measured preload, life, physical mass margin or event inspection signoff.

For accounting, use the same three incoming bound maps as the selected fixed harness and positive cover mounts. Add all eighteen new parts; replace the three drilled stock identities without crediting any chips. The case with an evidenced ≥400 g surrendered motor is the only case that leaves a positive estimated margin. The unmounted guide adds 55.08–76.68 g (65.88 g nominal) before its mounts and erases that small margin.
