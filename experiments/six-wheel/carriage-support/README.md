## What the force comparison establishes

The earlier low locks use Ø8 mm pins with an 18 mm bending arm. The historical stationary-hoist comparison reached about 441 MPa in the more heavily loaded pin and about 851 MPa if one side took the full vertical force. At the 58 mm shoe depth, the pitch had required roughly 8.35 kN of fore/aft couple. This result came from the compliant receiver's two signed station-drop trials. It remains specific to that experiment. [Original lock comparison](../fixed-hoist/lock-transfer.json) · [Signed contact and damper source](../shelf-structure/)

For the new bracket, the applied input contains both plunger forces once. The calculation transports the six-axis force and moment to the lock reference and applies the previously declared 140.21 N centered dead load. At every 31.25 µs clock it balances all three forces and three moments on two front and two rear supports. It tries 35%, 50% and 65% left share of front vertical load, with the unreported yaw exercised at ±50 N·m. The actual contact split and preload still require a fixture. The front pickup carries −1,240 to +126 N and the rear −571 to +185 N across the two histories and these assumptions. Downward is negative. The 2 mm section sweep over the forward arm gives 329.4 MPa maximum sampled longitudinal bending/axial stress. Using a 70 GPa elastic modulus, its normal-bending contribution alone ranges from 1.284 mm downward to 0.198 mm upward. This is comparable to the original guide clearance and exceeds the 0.10 mm unloaded absorber stand-off. A coupled replay with the attained boundary stiffness and strain/deflection measurements are required before transferring any capture result. Torsion, root and bridge rotation, thread compliance, fatigue and local stress concentrations remain open. The largest M6 equivalent stress in a tested missing-mast-bolt case is 440.4 MPa with 1.50 kN assumed assembly preload. That preload is a modeling input. The lower and upper chords see up to 4.95 and 0.595 kN compression. The native cut sections give pinned-pinned Euler screening loads of 9.66 and 1.13 kN at 190 mm effective span. The cantilever controls fall to 2.42 and 0.283 kN and fail. Losing the rear screw leaves as much as 185 N of rebound without the reviewed aft capture. The underlying mast-to-chassis splice also overloads when only one side reacts the shelf. Keep the original complete support requirement and verify the upper and lower load chains together. ## Machine, assemble and service

- Machine two integral brackets from certified 7075-T7351 aluminum with a minimum 400 MPa lot yield. Use the STEP's full sections and ≥4 mm main web. Keep the two top faces flat/coplanar to their separate Z412 and Z433 datums within 0.05 mm. Inspect the actual rib roots, internal tool radii, residual ligaments, stress risers and bare bearing transfer. The idealized cut corners require first-article review.
- Fit four M6 mast bolts per side between X−53/−35 and Z350/399. Match-drill both mast walls Ø10.10. Turn eight Ø10.00 (−0.05/+0) sleeves with Ø6.20 (+0.05/0) bores and individually face to the 25 mm nominal tube width, flush to 0.05 mm short. A sleeve projecting inside the rail interferes with the frontward bracket extraction. Hold coaxial mismatch to ≤0.10 mm before machining. The 43 mm finished underhead length uses M6×45 class 8.8 stock, a steel M6 washer and 10 AF class-8 locknut. Inspect actual thread engagement, metal-lot bearing and compressive stability.
- At X167 and |Y|182/197, drill the front bridge Ø5.50 and countersink from above 90° to Ø11.00. Individually gauge the received M5 flat-head profiles so the heads seat flush and make real conical contact. The CAD uses a Ø11 head and a 23 mm finished overall length. Fit one M5 washer and prevailing-torque nut underneath each. Check the minimum 2.0 mm remaining sheet (2.25 mm nominal) and the opening/outer edge ligaments. Reject proud or undersize heads and insufficient locking engagement. The tapped rear connection is one M6×25 class 8.8 bolt and washer per side at X−87, |Y|196. Tap M6×1 in the 21 mm deep crossbar from below, gauge at least 12 mm full usable thread and verify drill-bottom and screw-tip clearance. Establish a retained, qualified threadlocking process on representative aluminum and steel.
- Retract the unloaded slide, electrically isolate the robot, block the wheels and independently support the carriage before disturbing a joint. Keep both existing height locks installed. For each side remove four mast screws, the rear screw and both front screws. The front access study checks the 50 mm side leg of an angled driver and upward screw extraction on the retracted slide. Clear the crossbar flange then draw the released bracket 535 mm straight forward. The captured fasteners, sleeves, socket access and whole bracket have their own sweeps. Retain the supports until fit, torque retention and load sharing are verified. The earlier Z335 lower row swept into the fixed camera power lead; Z350 clears it without disturbing the harness. [Service and interference](fit.json)

The selected four machined predecessors are the two already-spliced mast tubes, the original low-lock crossbar and the absorber-drilled bridge. The remaining 50 solids comprise two brackets, eight sleeves, eight mast bolts with washers and nuts, four front bolts with washers and nuts and two rear screws with washers. Original CAD bodies or conservative reservations cover the sliding robot; the harness uses its registered straight and circular cable axes, and the absorber adjustment volume stays clear. Native penetration witnesses exercise both support seats, the front countersunk capture, rear retaining washer and mast sleeve load path. Bolt threads appear as major-diameter reservations. ## Weight and release gates

The installed checker charges **433.203–550.868 g** for the 50 articles, coatings, thread compound and markings, with zero drill-chip credit. The stationary-shaft robot, mast-to-rail splice and damped shelf reach **1,167 installed articles**. With matched shelf material its upper mass is 206.933 g over R103. Without credit for the old shelf material the upper excess is 1,127.401 g. A full group surrender, guide faces and their mounting are separate project choices. The physical inspection-scale margin is unknown. [Joined material and article comparison](accounting.json)

Fixture the robot with the service support engaged. Blue both seat faces, check preload/lock engagement, and instrument both brackets and original pins. Apply the signed reaction histories with reversal. Measure front and rear displacement, torsion and pin load at each side. Compare the observed load split, root/weak-chord strain and deflection with the model, then re-run the coupled impact before releasing a trajectory. Check fastener torque loss, hole elongation, permanent set and fretting after repeated reversals; weigh the final configuration.
