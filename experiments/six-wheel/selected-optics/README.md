## What the controller saw

Both simulated pickups contribute 246 fixed station captures each. At the reef there are 17 fixed release captures for A and 18 for B. The moving camera contributes 1,493 captures for A and 1,476 for B. Every finite interval encloses simulated truth. For each pipe the moving inverse correctly withholds bounds on five unbacked and seven unresolved captures. The registered station and reef geometry changes zero statuses or numerical bounds on 55 independently checked roof captures, including the settled first pipe at the second reef approach. [Field endpoint observations](roof-field.json) · [Full-view counts](observations.json)

For the moving slide, the study sweeps native occluders and tests each admissible bright/dark assignment on ambiguous pipe pixels. On A, 34 of 175 intermediate states have up to nine ambiguous pixels. All 3,309 assignments across the 175 states were checked. On B, 174 states have no ambiguous pixels. This assumes the modeled pipe-to-background contrast, static scene objects, calibrated rectified eyes and time-registered slide position. Lighting changes, reflections, motion blur, contamination and traffic require physical images. [Sweep implementation](stroke.py) · [Rendered interval checks](exposures_check.py)

## Reproduce and survey

Run the focused receipt checks with `python selected-optics/test_selected_optics.py -v`. The full regeneration command below needs the [registered shop artifacts](../selected-match/README.md), [local camera inputs](../camera-disconnects/README.md), [force and motion cache](../relieved-match/README.md), native field and lamp STEP files, the desktop JDK/Gradle cache, Agent FRC 0.22.1 and the generated receiver assembly map. The output directory holds depth images and full-resolution replay data. The scripts also update the source-controlled compact receipts.

```text
python selected-optics/run_study.py ARTIFACTS LAMP_STEP FIELD_STEP OUTPUT JDK GRADLE_HOME PLUGIN SELECTED_RECEIVER_MAP
```

For independent station landmark work, `scenes.py ARTIFACTS LAMP_STEP FIELD_STEP OUTPUT --stowed-robot-only` exports both robot-only fixed-eye grayscale and axial depth arrays plus their source receipt. Survey each camera with two independently located five-dot planes. `python selected-optics/survey.py roof 370` prints target coordinates. Supply the captured rectified two-eye measurements as a final JSON argument to test them. Repeat at 0, 185 and 370 mm and after removing and reinstalling the head. The project checks common origin, eye separation and uncertainty. Trials at both axial ends of the 1.50 mm barrel segment retained bounds at the critical B capture and at the sampled fixed-head captures. They do not establish a continuous pupil tolerance or substitute for measured optics. Measure timing, stereo association, contrast and coverage under actual field lighting.
