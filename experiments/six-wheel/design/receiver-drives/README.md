## Opposed cartridges

Each side has its own NEO 550, SPARK MAX, 68:13 UltraPlanetary and 1:1 pair of 22-tooth module-1 spur gears. The offset pair places the motor 22 mm outboard of the belt axis. Use a 550-compatible 13T input, matching REV input plate, output stage and an 8 mm working allocation for the 5 mm across-flats male hex. The shaft steps down to a 5 mm round journal. Verify actual socket engagement against the delivered output and retain shafts axially with a shoulder and keeper. A smooth envelope in the STEP reserves the commercial motor and gearbox. The upper motor surface is 98.5 mm above the carriage datum. Leave room for flexible leads and a vertical service pull with the lift lowered. ## Two-stage slide

One NEO 550 and SPARK MAX drive the middle rack through 68:13 followed by 76:21 UltraPlanetary cartridges (18.93:1 combined). A fixed 20T module-1 pinion, pitch radius 10 mm, pushes the middle-stage rack. A second pair of identical 20T pinions travels with that stage. Its rear pinion rolls along the fixed rack while its forward pinion acts on the underside of the outer-stage rack. They share a 480-3M-9 HTD loop on 20T pulleys, 210 mm center spacing. Thus 185 mm of middle travel produces 370 mm of outer travel. Separating the two rack lanes sideways prevents either pinion from picking up the wrong rack in stow. The original single-pinion concept lacked common rack overlap at full stroke.

The pitch lines are z = −116 and −96 mm about the synchronizing shafts at z = −106 mm. The driven rack pitch line is −90 above its −100 mm shaft. Each rack and involute pinion has 8 mm face width, 20° pressure angle, module 1 and 0.10 mm nominal total pitch backlash allocation. Actual root relief, heat treatment, tooth rating, gear quality, shaft retention and lubrication require fabrication review. The 320 mm nested rails retain 135 mm of overlap between adjacent stages at full reach. The two pairs of upper brackets cross above the fixed rail walls and attach to the middle rail's exposed top lips. Specify distortion-controlled welds and check rail straightness after fabrication. Local shelf tabs attach the upper rack. Validate actual threaded inserts or backing plates against pull-out loads before cutting the stock shelf.

- Set sync pretension initially to 55 N. Use matched straight offset bearing inserts in the rear middle-stage cheek holes. Changing X preserves rack pitch height. With the slide homed, loosen the front split hub, mesh both indexed racks, tension the belt and lock the hub with witness marks. At 25 N outer drag the nominal strand difference is about 27 N. Under a 140 N outer obstruction the calculated slack strand unloads. Stop on current/encoder disagreement. At full stroke the driven pinion retains over 16 mm before the rack's rear cut end.
- Measure true outer stroke with a TE/Celesco SP1-25 on the lift at y = −208 mm. The line runs 49–419 mm to an outer-stage tab, inside the 635 mm sensor range. Keep the line straight, strain-relieve wiring at the lift and inhibit movement on slack, snags or wiper dropout. The nominal ±0.25% full-scale number is already ±1.59 mm of the 2 mm pose allowance. Calibrate the installed full chain against independent travel measurements. Actual lead routing, folded controller harnesses and repeatability remain open. The contemporary manufacturer page reports limited availability. A period manufacturer data sheet shows the 25-inch family before kickoff. [TE part](https://www.te.com/en/product-SP1-25.html) · [Period Celesco sheet](https://intertechnology.com/Celesco/pdfs/SP1.pdf)
- The motor Hall signal measures commanded-drive rotation. The two passive pinions have independent AS5600 observations, with shared belt failure modes. Check outer travel against measured middle motion and the independent string pot. A positive-travel limit and a retract limit flank the middle dog. The two physical stops allow about 1 mm extra middle travel, equivalent to about 2 mm extra outer travel. Retain the string pot as the independent outer-stage check. Hardware default is neutral on disable, stale data or jam. Cable drag and tip moments can still move an unpowered mechanism.
- Use 5 mm journal shafts and two 5 × 13 × 5 mm bearings per timing axle. At 25 N friction and the candidate acceleration the overhung 14.5 mm front pinion has roughly 33 MPa nominal bending stress in a 5 mm circular section, before the shock factor or stress concentrations. Validate the actual shoulder/split-hub and shaft hardness. Use restrained shaft collars and a replaceable non-contact guard. Align pitch centers and verify free-running backlash over full travel before adding belt preload.

## Two-piece replay and electrical budget

The [study](sizing.json) reruns the original 1 ms registered transfers at −180 and +180 mm and continues each identified pipe into modeled paired trough-floor/lip support. It replaces the earlier commanded lateral normals with independently compressed springs and viscous damping. With μ = 0.4, 10 N nominal preload and a 40 ms release-speed ramp, both reach the sustained contact. Peak belt force is 4.00 N per side. Actual modeled normals range from 9.906 to 10.103 N, and maximum lateral cartridge motion is 0.044 mm. With 53.5 mm assumed bore radius, the trailing rim leaves the straight strand at approximately u=152.92 mm. Belt force there becomes zero. The pipe coasts the last 12 mm to the shelf lip with assumed shelf friction 0.10. Both routes also reach sustained paired contact at 0.35 shelf friction. The model tapers normal load over the last 1 mm of straight contact. A further study must establish actual local pressure, friction, edge response and full-robot clearance.

| Load case | Model result at 9.5 V bus |
| --- | --- |
| Release spin-up | 5,492 motor rpm. At an assumed 3 × 10⁻⁶ kg·m² input-referred inertia, 40 ms ramp, 4.0 N working force and 0.4 N parasitic drag, one grip requires about 6.8 A phase and 6.72 V. With 90% assumed controller efficiency this is about 5.35 A bus per side. The proposed 8 A phase cap fails at 7 × 10⁻⁶ kg·m². Measure inertia and temperature before adopting it. |
| Slide trajectory | 240 mm in 2 s and 370 mm in 3 s yield peak outer speeds of 225 and 231 mm/s. At 25 N outer drag the drive needs about 6.0 A phase, 2.9 V, 2.0 A bus. The slower 150 mm/s withdrawal needs separate encoder tracking within the existing 15 mm tolerance. At 140 N drag the proposed 20 A phase limit and slack-side tension both fail. |
| Counterexamples | Under 2 N nominal preload and μ = 0.08 both routes lose the piece over the rear edge. Removing damping exceeds the physical ±1.5 mm travel at every timestep tested. The undamped failure mode changes with timestep. The dry damped 0.25, 0.5, 1 and 2 ms trials all reach first paired contact, with 0.0425–0.046 mm maximum carriage motion. This supports a damping experiment, without assigning a measured coefficient. |

Record per-phase bus voltage, drive/controller current, measured belt speed, forces and outer/middle position. The proposed limits are 8 A phase per grip and 20 A phase for the slide. The POC uses no assigned CAN IDs, hardware gains or deployed adapter. The incremental BOM is per body. Allow another 0.5 kg on each translating stage for wiring, closures, clips, seals and omitted detail. Include three remote SPARK MAX controllers and protected wiring separately in the central electrical BOM. A two-pipe mechanical replay reproduces geometric support. The independent stereo/lip study rerun on these two dry trajectories gives 14/17 guaranteed shelf-withdrawal phases at 0.10 px, zero at 0.25 px, and first positive capture 36–52 ms after stock shelf truth. Whole-robot departure, earned score and physical acceptance need separate evidence.

The [native checks](checks.json) reopen the named STEP layer and original package. At four named poses, no positive-volume pair intersections were measured. Nine additional mesh positions span 0–370 mm, including fractional tooth phases. Opposite ±1.5 mm compliance extremes also clear in the placing pose. Smooth belt/pulley-root and supplier envelopes prove space claims at the sampled positions. Manufacturing tolerances, guard dimensions, calculated tooth contact stress, true bearing life and intersample clearance need separate review.

Each revised riser and long screw shares its original shelf bolt hole and insert. A Ø7 mm vertical driver above the two rear screws strikes the gear, shaft, mount, gearbox and motor. Isolate and remove the rear drive stack first. Both front drivers clear the short cartridge at the reviewed place pose. Unload and retain the preload assembly before removing the side frames. Lower and support the carriage for disassembly, remove the original four fasteners, retain matched inserts and springs by side, then reassemble and verify preload, encoder phase, force and actual travel. Add axial keepers, wire clamps, removable guards and identifiable witness marks to the manufacturing drawing. A 3-D service corridor does not verify a human grip, a particular tool or trained pit time.

## Retained long-belt counterexample

The [prior 537 mm belt revision](https://github.com/ehartye/frc-2025-poc/tree/f830676c9103448f2f00a0f424395c1bf312078a/design/receiver-drives) passed its nominal transfer. In the registered departure with the admissible inward cartridge and +2 mm slide discrepancy, the real hollow-pipe solid intersected each belt envelope by 143.404 mm³ at the stock shelf 1 mm witness, 144.069 mm³ at +15 ms and 103.392 mm³ at +30 ms. The fixed risers had zero intersection. This result drove the shorter work surface and explicit coast. The 50 ms legacy command delay alone does not verify a particular whole-pipe/robot departure.

The independent 361-body short-package check then used the new passive grip and coast trajectory. At the sampled poses with the +2 mm forward discrepancy and both movable cartridges displaced inward by 1.5 mm, the finite pipe wall had no positive-volume overlap. The conservative support sweep bounded whole-body separation only after +47 ms, with a later minimum gap of 35.13 mm. Stereo against the 537 mm front plane detected clearance in all 17 phases (55–71 ms after shelf truth at ±0.10 px), but the current pad-to-withdrawal timing budget admits **zero whole-robot phases**. Keep the controller inhibited for this evidence class pending a validated whole-body timing interval or changed measurement. The 14/17 result above applies only to the shelf.
