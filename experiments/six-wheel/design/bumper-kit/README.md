## Cut and build one installed set

Robot +X is forward, +Y is left and Z is height above the design floor. The following are finished dimensions in mm. Build two interchangeable sets for fast color changes. Test the mount on each set individually.

| Article | Cut or purchase for one set | Fabrication and assembly |
|---|---|---|
| Welded aluminum backing | Two end faces 710 × 138 × 4 and four returns 350 × 138 × 4, from 5052 sheet | End faces run completely across both returns. Each return occupies X from 1 to 351 in its own half. Butt weld on a square fixture; dress the inner weld so the 700 mm frame inserts freely. Installed inner flat is at 351, outer flat at 355. Recheck roll, diagonals and the 2 mm bare return seam after welding. |
| Contact strips | Two 648 × 86 × 1 and four 348 × 86 × 1 aluminum strips | Fixed to the inner backing at Z57–143, flush at the frame face. Drill 8.6 through at each anchor. Back up the sheet with the frame wall on straight runs. Refit with transfer dye after welding; shim the actual tube flatness while respecting the outer hull. |
| Threaded bosses | Eight aluminum Ø24 × 8 disks | Weld concentric to the outer backing before foam installation. Drill and tap the completed 12 mm stack M8×1.25; match-mark each screw's engagement. CAD uses the nominal threaded interface. Verify pullout, thread condition, locking method, torque and repeatability with production stock. |
| Chassis crush sleeves | Eight OD12, ID8.6 aluminum tubes; four 20 long, four 25 long | Jig-bore both tube walls Ø12 coaxially, square sleeve flush, weld or mechanically retain with qualified process. | Fasteners | Four ISO 4762 M8×35 at ends, four M8×40 at sides; eight OD16 × 1.6 washers | Heads face the chassis interior. Eight holes share Z127. End holes lie at Y=±175, in the X-normal cross tubes. Side holes lie at X=±125, in the Y-normal drive tubes. The native API applies the cut to the previously bearing-machined rails. Keep spare hardware and a 6 mm hex driver accessible on the cart. On a supported, powered-off robot, approach the screw heads from the open underside, remove the screws/washer pairs and slide each U outward 440 mm along X. Mark front, rear and side position on hidden inward cloth. The proposed two-person change must still be timed.

## Geometry and loads

The square face has 69.5 mm of PE, Z43–181. The deepest local hardware projects 13.4 mm beyond the frame and retains over 61 mm of foam in front of the screw tip. Both plane end and face-on corner coverage retain at least 57.15 mm of solid foam. The outer diagonal corner relief limits even Euclidean distance from the actual frame corner to 100.57 mm. The charted field hull therefore remains conservative at the corners. Use an 850 mm go/no-go installed gauge including cloth, folds and numbers on both sets; a positive manufacturing overage requires trimming or a fresh field registration before reusing path margins.

Normal contact goes from foam to the welded backing and the contact strips against the rail flats. An illustrative 1 kN pull-away load concentrated at one M8 station would put 27.3 MPa on its 36.6 mm² tensile stress area. A 1 kN tangential load applied entirely to one thread root gives 27.3 MPa nominal shear and 31.3 MPa bearing on the 4 mm plate at Ø8, before any load sharing. These are static demands. Joint preload, welded rail strength, wood-free backer flatness, thread stripping, corner weld fatigue, dynamic impact, wear and foam recovery need component and assembled tests. The available manufacturer foam coupon data do not establish full-robot impact resistance.
