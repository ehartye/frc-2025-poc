## Power and communications

Use one bolt-terminal EnerSys NP18-12, insulated 6 AWG copper supply and return, one Anderson SB50 pair and a single listed Cooper Bussmann CB185-120 ahead of the REV-11-1850 PDH. The robot side of the SB pair stays onboard. The battery-side exclusion has a 12 in per-leg allowance in R103. Bind the battery with a removable positive restraint in every direction, shield positive and return terminals, isolate both poles from chassis and expose the disconnect and fuses for inspection. Specify one prepared conductor per distribution terminal. Cable clamps carry harness strain independently of electrical terminals.

## Moving wiring and observations

Drive phases are budgeted as 12 AWG, 0.70 m per side. Lift uses 12 AWG at 0.75 m. Slide uses 14 AWG at 2.10 m and grips 16 AWG at 2.65/2.85 m. Three factory phases and a separate encoder cable run to each motor. The simulation represents the conducting two-wire path and adds 3 mΩ contact resistance to each cable loop. Keep the protective branch copper through the motor circuit to the extent required by R622, with factory lead exceptions verified against the actual device and 2025 rule. Keep flexible phase looms away from analog/RC sense lines. Select continuous-flex cable with an actual bend-radius and cycle rating. Use guarded vertical and horizontal loops with keyed removable terminals, positive anchors at frame, carriage and shelf, and slack at both independent stroke endpoints. No bend radius or connector strain relief has been qualified by collision results.

Seat and Entry retain the +40/+100 mm receiver datums. At a seated transverse pipe center +80 mm and R57.15 mm, the reflecting underside is **38.33 / 25.61 mm** from a nominal aperture sensor plane 22 mm below the shelf top. The 30 mm range of the first 4301 candidate misses Seat. The selected QTRXL-HD-01RC 4501 provides a catalog range up to 80 mm on reflective material. Its 5 × 20 mm board sits below each port, with the emitter registered to the aperture. Each takes up to 32 mA. Use two timed RC channels with 3.3 V supply/logic, verified current budget and a common reference. Calibrate open, dark, dusty, tilted, disconnected and moving-pipe cases on both channels. A high signal or a channel reset cannot by itself assign custody. [Dimension and range calculation](inspection.json) · [Sensor specification](https://www.pololu.com/product/4501/specs)

Each of the lift/slide endpoints and both pawl positions gets its own monitored NC contact. Instrument the direct TE SP1-25 string potentiometer on roboRIO AI0, with measured excitation and endpoint calibration. The four AS5600 analog angles use a second separately addressed ADS1015 on the observer. Keep the fast three-pad scan on its original converter, qualify scheduling on the shared host bus, and timestamp each channel. Disable a new move on inconsistent switch states, lost encoder turn counts, stale analog frames, disagreement between direct and motor-derived travel, or ambiguous clearance. Single NC contact wiring does not diagnose every short; establish fresh transitions and independent mechanical stops. Select Omron D2F-01L-D3 gold-contact hinge-lever switches. A proposed isolated/buffered 5 V sensing interface supplies at least the catalog 1 mA microload and presents compatible roboRIO logic. Six channels at 2 mA each add 12 mA to the sensing budget. Verify actual operating force, overtravel, noise, open/short detection, mounting and the second ADC carrier before build. The Pi, OAK-D-LITE, original pressure-pad ADC and its two regulated supplies follow the existing observer package. ## Shared rail and thermal screening

A 20 ms phase replay, with both endpoints of each acceleration, cruise and braking interval, computes traction in every registered differential segment. It includes the lift's worst effort over each relevant compound event, both active grips throughout loaded travel and relevant receiver events, a simultaneous slide drive on moves containing retraction or extension, 25 W observer, 15 W roboRIO, 12 W radio, controller housekeeping, 24 V valve power and a continuous 15 A compressor stress. Each branch supplies its electrical power at its own solved terminal voltage. The model counts braking effort as a draw and takes no regeneration credit. Each branch has hot-resistance allowance of 1.25 times nominal copper plus contact resistance. The common pack/trunk resistance is 17/8 mΩ. Limits must be rechecked with the actual battery, wire lengths, terminal drops, device resets and compressor start-up.

| Parked-partner reference case | Duration | Current peak | Minimum PDH / device | Drive phase peak |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Registered timing at 62 kg | 114.213 s | 83.63 A | 10.31 / 9.82 V | 35.78 A |
| Same spin ramp at 67 kg | 114.213 s | See calculation | Phase current exceeds proposed 38 A | 38.45 A |
| 67 kg, spin-wheel acceleration reduced from 0.70 to 0.64 m/s² | 114.942 s | 86.68 A | 10.23 / 9.71 V | 36.72 A |

The 67 kg case covers the assembled upper estimate plus pipe (63.04 kg). The 62 kg baseline alone did not cover it. The revised route uses 1.082 Ah of modeled terminal current and produces 2.54 kJ pack and 1.20 kJ trunk resistive heat, over one two-trip mission. These values exclude extra time waiting for moving traffic. Device temperature, current-limit response time, hot resistance, charge acceptance, pack aging and thermal-breaker trip/recovery still require measurements. The per-branch results distinguish input I²R loss, two conducting phase-wire losses and the equivalent DC winding loss. They omit thermal capacitance, insulation limits, inverter thermal transients, airflow and winding-to-case impedance. They cannot qualify repeated matches. In the 11.8 V/32 mΩ weak-source case with increased slide drag the lowest device sees 8.05 V. The 10.9 V/40 mΩ case loses the stable model operating point on the first approach. Actual hardware would change load, limit or reset. [Equations and negative controls](replay.py) · [Complete per-event and per-device receipts](power.json)

## Pawl, stored air and gravity

The onboard VIAIR 90C, hard-mounted 125 psi relief, Nason SM-2B-115R/443 upstream switch into PH, 574 mL AVT-PP-35 tank, high-side gauge, accessible total vent, a single adjustable relieving regulator and low-side gauge form the air source. Use R804-compatible rated fittings and 1/4 in OD tubing. Limit storage to 120 psi and working pressure to 60 psi. The candidate release calculation uses **50 psi**. Reserve am-3871a relief, am-4223 gauge, am-4218a regulator/gauge or documented equivalent, and a full-circuit red-labeled accessible manual dump. Confirm both gauges drop to zero with the dump operated. Do not trap pressure behind checks or blocked exhaust ports. Exact cylinder and fittings pressure ratings, compressor inrush, regulator relief operation, gauge positions, anti-rotation, dead volume and field compliance remain open. [2025 R801–814](SOURCES.md)

Specify a spring-return Mead M4V210-06-24VDC 5/2 valve with 1/8 NPT and 3 W coil, PH channel 0 selected to 24 V. Select the actual port routing so the cylinder's release chamber vents when the coil de-energizes, the mechanical plunger/spring drives engagement without relying on stored air, and both chambers can vent during full-system dump. At 50 psi the full 12 mm bore can produce at most **39.0 N** ideal. Retraction-side force is lower and depends on the rod, seal friction, linkage and remaining pressure. The 10 mm stroke displaces 1.131 mL per full-bore stroke before lines and dead volume. This is an actuator upper bound, not a holding rating. Confirm the actual assembled exhaust schematic, return spring and engagement feedback with no supply and with residual air.

The drum running line lies on the −X side of its horizontal +Y axis. Winding the loaded carriage up pulls the vertical reach downward. Under the open 1:1 transfer, gravity drives the shaft +Y, counterclockwise viewed from the +Y gearbox output. Assemble the maker's **CCW-lock** configuration only after verifying that datum against the built rope route. The four external lands in the 31 mm am-4398 ratchet sit on the input side of both reduction stages; the assembly directions show the input gear, carrier and unchanged downstream gearbox. A measured 16:1 installation would reflect the 2.859 N·m drum static proof target to 0.179 N·m ideal at the ratchet. Even at the largest gear radius, tangential reaction is at least 11.5 N. A fully engaged flank carries the load. Cylinder force by itself cannot certify static holding, tooth contact, pin shear, spring return, fastener load or geartrain integrity.

The worst one-land wait at the 11.5824 mm drum radius is **1.137 mm** of additional vertical movement. At 0.253 m/s downward travel and a 25 ms mechanical response, gravity plus one land permits 10.53 mm before contact and 1.70 J of translational impact energy. With 1 mm assumed arrest compression, mean drum torque is **21.15 N·m**, 7.4 times static proof. At 50 ms it reaches 43.34 N·m. Shaft and reflected rotor energy can increase demand. No product-specific allowable capture impulse or measured mechanism delay supports a fail-safe claim. Preserve a mechanical service pin and independent gravity restraint for maintenance. A demonstrated bound on failed-to-engage, single-rope failure and loaded emergency catch is needed; a failed test calls for an independently rated anti-drop device or changed mission. [Source drawings and installation](SOURCES.md) · [Impact and delay screen](pawl.json)

The offline interlock requires an observed stationary and independently supported load before it requests pneumatic release. It permits descent only after actual released-position feedback. When stopping, it holds the lift, confirms stationary support, vents the release valve, waits for stable engaged feedback, then removes motor support. Disabled, stale, contradicting, expired and failed-pressure states revoke motion and de-energize the valve. A latched fault requires explicit inspection before rearm. Upstream callers must provide debounced, fresh switch states and measured timeouts. Independent load evidence, physical stop/lock dwell, switch placement and sensing of actual tension are not commissioned. A lost supply can defeat motor support immediately. ## Fit and event dossier

The upright 181 × 76 × 167 mm pack exceeded the original battery height. The 225.4 mm PDH exceeded the old 160 mm distribution reservation. The revised tray sides and hold-down, distribution bridge, fixed electronics platform and three moving harness anchors are original parametric geometry. The reviewed service volumes cover vertical battery removal after detaching the strap, PDH breaker extraction and main disconnect access. A first layout put the breaker into the tray. A second shelf anchor fit the four discrete poses but crossed the synchronization bracket at 90–120 mm extension. Moving the anchor toward the center then hit the slide motor. The final local minimum (75, −45, −90) preserves a 16 × 10 mm underside mounting face. Harness flex, RF shadowing, real connectors, mounting screws and a technician's hand still need checking. Use the enclosing radio and roboRIO volumes for packaging only. [All parts, geometry and service paths](package.py) · [Four-pose receipt](packaging.json)
