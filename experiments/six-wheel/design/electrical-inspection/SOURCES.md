# Electrical and inspection source register

[Omron D2F microload contact tables and terminal variants](https://components.omron.com/us-en/system/files/2023-01/datasheet_pdf/B036-E1.pdf) support D2F-01L-D3 as the NC contact candidate. Qualify the lever against mechanical actuation and the 5 V/1 mA reference against its interface. [REV PDH connector and thermal tables](https://docs.revrobotics.com/ion-control/pdh/specs) constrain the proposed branch distribution.

The historical authority is the official English 2025 manual. The construction section is marked **V11**. Its standalone PDF had SHA-256 `1c44d554f10be3ffe32f3bb45cbcae353707cb141125f703d13f8c898058b15f` when checked on 2026-11-18. The event checklist was Rev. 2. Recheck applicability against the specific event date and updates. Supplier dimensions and current catalog configurations establish candidate fit only. Original box, cylinder and bracket geometry was authored here. No manufacturer drawings or vendor CAD are distributed.
