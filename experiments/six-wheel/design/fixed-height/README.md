The carriage can stay at **560 mm** for both station intake and L1 placement if the robot receives with the shelf retracted and extends it **370 mm** only at the target. This removes vertical travel from the station cycle. The software trial retains separate gates for sensing, release, withdrawal and inspection. A second configuration locks both axes at **940 mm** for the straight LEAVE/PARK role. Both are project designs that a team can choose before a match. ## Fix both sides directly to the masts

The [low placement STEP](low-locks.step) contains 38 independently reopened physical solids. The [high STEP](locks.step) contains 50. Both include the edited members. The crossbar transfers vertical load to two stationary masts through new steel pins and crush sleeves. No load credit is assigned to the motor, ratchet, gearbox, common drum shaft or ropes. Permanently isolate lift actuation in either fixed configuration. The high version also pins each telescoping channel with a headed clevis pin, washer and formed-end keeper. The low version retains horizontal actuation for placement. A configuration change requires a supported pit rebuild with the corresponding parts and control configuration. [Editable shop geometry](native.py) · [Native contacts and dimensions](native.json)

- Bore the solid crossbar for each 8 mm smooth pin at Y ±222.5 mm and Z equal to carriage height minus 137.5 mm. An 8.2 mm clearance bore meets the pin. The 12 mm OD × 8.2 mm ID sleeves bridge the 50 mm hollow mast and 7 mm shoe gap. A matching relief clears the nut at the shoe face. The polymer shoe carries no credited clamping load. Screw threads, locknut engagement, bearing surfaces and actual fit need shop confirmation.
- Four vertical M6 through-bolts join the bridge and structural crossbar at X −87 and Y ±128 and ±145 mm. Each outer slide rail has two 100 mm long, 3 mm leg fillet-weld envelopes on its outer faces. The unmodified bridge and the light CB-1 replacement are distinct parts; this study includes the original plate. Qualify the aluminum joint fusion, heat-affected material, flatness and slide alignment. A trial fixing at Y +100 crossed the synchronizer cheek and belt in retraction. A height pin at Y ±214 collided with the existing carriage bolts.
- With the haul connection unavailable, a simulated 1 mm vertical displacement produces material contact through the crossbar/pin, pin/sleeve and sleeve/mast interfaces on both sides. The high horizontal lock likewise blocks 1 mm of rack travel. Nominal diametral clearances allow up to 0.25 mm of cumulative centered vertical take-up before elasticity and tolerances. Under a one-sided 3g comparison load, the modeled demand is 367 N and nominal pin bending is 131 MPa on an 18 mm cantilever. The same calculation reaches 438 MPa at 10g. Neither is a measured collision load. Unequal seating, bending, local wall/ligament fatigue, weld failure, lock/keeper loss, guide behavior with one failed side and an actual peak load still need a captive proof fixture. Loss of both positive locks or their common crossbar remains hazardous. The low slide also needs positive vertical retention and load qualification on its own moving rails.
- In the low configuration, 157 moving-source envelopes clear the new fixed hardware throughout 0–370 mm of horizontal travel. The full robot is 850 × 850 × 1,059 mm retracted, including bumpers. The fixed structural frame perimeter remains 2,800 mm against R104’s 3,048 mm limit. Height leaves 7.8 mm against 1,066.8 mm. At target the front reaches +535 mm, 185 mm beyond the +350 mm frame face, within the 457.2 mm R105 extension limit. ## Reproduce

Run from the project root with OpenCascade-capable Python, a JDK and the installed plugin. Save native receipts under `artifacts/fixed-height`. Use `--low receive` for the retracted 560 mm build and `--low place` for its native placement. Repeat the two alliance variants. `native.py` with no `--low` checks the 940 mm fully locked configuration, and `--counterexample` recovers the conflicting through-hole locations.

```powershell
python design/fixed-height/native.py red artifacts/fixed-height/low-red-place.json --low place --step artifacts/fixed-height/low-locks.step
python design/fixed-height/function.py
python design/fixed-height/ports.py --java-home "$env:JAVA_HOME"
python design/fixed-height/ports.py --java-home "$env:JAVA_HOME" --counterfeed .65 --output design/fixed-height/ports-065.json
python design/fixed-height/clearance.py --java-home "$env:JAVA_HOME"
python design/fixed-height/handoff.py
python design/fixed-height/field.py
python design/fixed-height/review.py --plugin-root "$env:AGENT_FRC_PLUGIN_ROOT"
```

The dedicated low configuration also has a reviewed third-motor omission with a dimensioned long-block input cap, two-sided lock checks and separate grouped-versus-weighed mass cases. [Fixed-height motor omission](../../fixed-lift-omission/README.md)
