## An independent catch during travel

A dedicated carriage-mounted pinion following a rack on the stationary mast observes true carriage travel even when all four haul lines stay taut. The preferred *study architecture* combines a spring-applied, mechanically latched rail brake with a carriage-driven centrifugal trip that can override energized release. Loss of release power applies the brake even on a slow descent. A lower-speed runaway under power stays undetected until the threshold. A separate positively monitored park remains necessary. Use a separate stationary reaction member with independently rated attachment. Neither the original hauling shaft nor its encoder supplies the trigger or carries the stopping load.

A 12 mm pitch-radius follower, 4:1 speed increase, 12 g weight and 12.5 mm weight radius generate 1.07 N at 0.253 m/s versus 2.04 N at the hypothetical 0.35 m/s trip. Spring, friction, vibration, vertical shock and disengagement hysteresis must allow this narrow separation. A 24-position arrest ring at the follower adds up to 3.14 mm index travel. Allowing gravity during index and actuator delay gives **16.80 mm at 10 ms and 55.45 mm at 50 ms** assuming 2 g *net* deceleration. A rigid dog cannot itself deliver this assumed controlled deceleration; qualify a progressive brake/energy-absorbing element and its force curve, or redesign. Upper moving mass gives about 359 N steady reaction during that illustrative deceleration, before impact peaks. Both timings exceed the 5 mm place clearance. This rack/governor still needs a selected brake, supplier mounting drawing, drive reserve, packaging for the complete stroke and a tested free-fall trigger.

## Release evidence

1. **Supplier review.** Identify exact revision, minimum mass, speed range, permitted static dwell, return and usable stroke. Submit both energy extremes, one-sided case, slow descent, partially compressed case and a controller continuing to drive down. Obtain reaction force/time traces, replacement duty and shutdown conditions. Include actual metric part availability at the season boundary.
2. **Independent captive fixture.** Measure both masses, center of mass, minimum end clearance, unilateral plate travel, shaft/line payout and static load path. A qualified reviewer must approve guarded containment, secondary support and actual proof load before dynamic tests.
3. **Independent trigger.** Verify normal descending speed and nuisance trips at the high vibration boundary. With ropes remaining taut, observe lift-triggered application on simulated shaft failure. Separately exercise power loss at rest, under-speed creep, stuck release, partial reset and sensor disagreement. Measure travel to rest, peak mount loads, holding after loss of power and absence of rebound/restart. Require deliberate supported reset.
4. **Low end.** Measure arrival before, during and after end-stop engagement, with minimum and maximum payload and adverse side loading. Record both damper strokes and bracket deflections. Reject contact with either fixed service arm, partial stroke beyond supplier data, failed side share, inadequate protection on one unit or a field/piece contact preceding arrest. Verify actual gaps and force margins under tolerance.
5. **Successor registration.** Separate moving/fixed masses and re-run whole-robot insertion/service, continuous pose, mission, field and sensor tests if installed. Keep this isolated proposal and the 545 source as distinct builds.
