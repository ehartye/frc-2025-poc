What keeps the axle in place?

The gearbox drives a rounded hex shaft. A radial clip limits outward travel, and a screw and shim limit inward travel. Follow the center axle from the wheel to the outside of the frame.

Set the bearing keeper

Machine four holes on a 37.80 mm bolt circle at 14°, 104°, 194° and 284°. The M3 × 8 screws sit flush. The 3.10 mm cover has a Ø26.80 opening and a Ø32.00 × 1.65 pocket for the flange.

The cover bears on the stationary outer rim. The rotating shim has a Ø15.00 to Ø16.70 annular nose. Check both faces on the received bearing.

Check the groove and play

Turn the groove to Ø11.43 × 1.03 mm wide with R0.10 roots. The inboard flank is 12.81 mm from the shaft’s outer end. A 2.18 mm thrust washer gives about 0.15 mm outward axle travel before the modeled stop.

Measure the actual ring, groove, endplay and remaining socket engagement. A seated clip, a free-spinning shaft and repeatable removal are required at the bench.
