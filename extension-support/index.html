Follow the two sliding rails as they carry the shelf and a seated pipe. The bearing patches move while the shock absorbers stay on the chassis.

    What went into the calculation?

    One previously seated pipe, the two shelf layers and four moving rails supply the static load. Green bars show CAD floor contact after 1.05 mm downward trial displacement. The fixture idealizes each moving joint as a rigid plane with two elastic contact stations. At each station the nominal stiffness is 10,000 N/mm. The drawn vertical scale separates the stages for readability.
