The custom hub fits the six existing axial fasteners at its measured wheel phase with 0.087 mm nominal radial clearance. The nearest fork-side part is 0.125 mm away. The exact half-inch tread screws bottom with 1.5 mm belt compression. A documented three-eighths-inch REV-29-2941 leaves 6.26 mm engagement in blue 5994K9 and 6.51 mm in black 5994K1. Under a cut-ply net-section assumption the blue option offers 85.4 N and black 128.1 N at the seam. Their corresponding per-wheel motor demand is 122.7 and 123.5 N. Neither margin supports a release without actual contact and failure data. All twelve phase, compression and source-stock scenarios are in the private `seam-options.json` receipt.
