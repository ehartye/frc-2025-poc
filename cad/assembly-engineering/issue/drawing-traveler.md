# Fixed-low front jack assembly traveler

Dimensions are in millimetres in the robot frame. Positive X points forward, positive Y left and positive Z up. Use the [neutral part set](station-and-jack-interfaces.step) together with the [named changed and retired parts](interfaces.json) and the original [complete shallow-cage assembly](../../shallow-cage/reopen.json). Keep the fixture constrained at the parked lift and retracted receiver until the module tools are removed.
