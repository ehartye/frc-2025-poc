# Battery bay · matched stops and insulated keeper

Fit this revision to the fixed-low chassis after machining its lower deck and before installing the side electronics. Coordinates are millimetres in the parked robot. The STEP contains the changed battery articles. The [predecessor assembly](../issue/interfaces.json) supplies the eight cap screws, lower backing washers and nuts, tray, deck and breaker. ## Receive the correct case

Purchase the EnerSys Genesis NP18-12B with upright M5 bolt-through tabs. Record the actual suffix and guard height. The model-specific US-NP18-12B-01 drawing specifies 76.2 ± 1 in width, 181 ± 1 in length and 166 ± 1 in height to the highest molded guard. The later North American family table gives 76 × 181 × 167 with a ±2 mm allowance. The interchangeable stop set accepts their combined 74–78 × 179–183 × 165–169 mm case envelope. The family’s 6.17 kg typical ±5% permits 6.479 kg for the dressed robot’s bare battery allocation. The removable battery is excluded from inspection weight. The tab locations, lid recesses and plastic guards differ substantially from the flat demonstration block. [Original EnerSys drawing](https://www.aeroelectric.com/Mfgr_Data/Batteries/Enersys_Hawker/NP18_12B.pdf) · [Current EnerSys family sheet](https://www.enersys.com/4a1436/globalassets/documents/product-documentation/genesis/np/amer/amer-en-rs-np_0625.pdf)

## Make the stop set

1. Cut two end and two side angles in 6061-T6. Locate the interchangeable pocket about X−212, Y−14.5, leaving 0.90 mm nominal per face with the largest accepted case. Match-bore each foot Ø4.5 to the two existing holes in the main deck and tray. The end stops are 45 mm tall from the tray, with 3 mm upright and foot thickness. The side stops reach 10 mm. The STEP includes the paired cap-head clearances. The interchangeable set retains case clearance at the tolerance extremes; use a matched replacement set for a dedicated smaller pack. Dimension that set to the measured width and length plus 1.80 mm overall. Hold the inner faces to ±0.10 mm and measure the actual case to ±0.05 mm.
2. At X−212, drill Ø5.60 through the end feet at Y−116 and +86. Relieve the rear foot around the breaker to X−236 and Y−114 on its affected corner. Keep the section continuous between the rod and both mounting screws. Install the eight original M4 anchors with their backing washers and prevailing nuts before wiring. Remove burrs on the battery side.
3. Cut two Ø5 threaded rods to 197.00 mm from 200 mm stock. Retain the lower washers/locknuts. Slip a Ø8.00 OD / Ø5.40 ID 6061 compression sleeve over each rod. Finish each sleeve to 166.80 mm between the 158 mm foot top and the 324.80 mm keeper underside. The two sleeves establish the height independently of the battery lid.
4. Cut the one-piece insulating keeper from 3/16 in Norplex NP510A FR-4. Transfer the two Ø5.60 holes at 202 mm spacing. Deburr, seal the cut edges using the shop's laminate treatment and inspect for edge delamination. Place the keeper flat on both sleeves, then the top washers and locking nuts. Match the upper guard profile with a nonconductive transfer template before loading. Keep the ports, tabs and lead boots clear of the cover and its two transverse fingers. [Material specification](https://www.norplex-micarta.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/TDB_NP510A.pdf)

## Service and acceptance

Isolate and disconnect the red keyed SB50. Release both battery leads from fixed restraints. Remove the two upper M5 nuts, washers, keeper and removable spacer sleeves; the steel tie rods remain in the robot. Lift 250 mm vertically, then carry the pack wholly past either side of the bumper. Use the registered grip and upper-accessory gauges when specifying the actual insulated pulling fixture. The 172 mm rise from the earlier straight extraction test left a tie rod in the lateral exit path. Check unloaded service on both sides with the dressed battery and complete lead pair before use.

The issued bay checks metal interference, supplier case envelopes, matched lateral clearances and external access. A separate check must establish where the keeper catches load on actual reinforced ABS guard surfaces under inversion, the allowable bearing and travel there, the terminal/boot recess geometry, cable bend/strain relief and the finished connector fastening. The initial supplier drawing gives overall guard height and omits the complete upper surface or a rated clamping pad. A clear service envelope alone gives no load rating. The CAD archive linked from the assembly page preserves the previous bay until a successor master is issued.


The eight feet seat on the tray. A 0.01 mm compression probe on all eight stacks gives 22.580 mm² per screw head, 47.713 mm² per underside washer and 27.913 mm² per nut. The tray bears on 43,409.249 mm² of deck. The deck has two crossrail lands of 8,118.912 and 8,179.427 mm² and six M5 ties with 381.690 mm² per anchor. All six upper socket approaches clear the parked robot. These areas establish the modeled compression path. Fastener preload, slip at impact, shelf stress and cell-guard resistance need separate verification. [Independent installed reconstruction](tray-to-frame.json)

Use terminal axes on the negative-X side (X−234.8) with positive toward Y−87 and negative toward Y+58. Ø20 mm vertical keepouts above both posts clear the keeper. Rotating the case 180° puts both posts at X−189.2, where the keeper covers the entire Ø12 gauge. This is an obstruction check. The horizontal M5 terminal screw, lug, boot and lead still need a registered assembly. Both sleeves and the rigid keeper have a separate clear vertical removal above the entire robot.
