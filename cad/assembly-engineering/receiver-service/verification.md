# Receiver service assembly

The corrected receiver, matched battery bay and upper camera cable support share one 1,268-solid engineering assembly. All coordinates use the team’s shop drawing frame. The purchase register counts the two SB50 housings within the base harness, and the separate materials register assigns each housing its own geometry. The assembled estimate is 52.13977 kg against the 52.16312 kg R103 limit. The 23.36 g margin is conditional on the included 5 kg unresolved stock allowance and the stated receiving bounds.

Anderson's standard SB50 family file from its red housing listing, dated 2008, differs from a simple 35.1 mm-wide rectangular proxy. Its casting spans 36.51 mm in width and has Ø3.658 mounting bores at 19.05 mm spacing. A 180° front-to-back mate at the 81.30 mm catalog pair length shows 17.19 mm³ of overlap on these family solids; +0.25 mm longitudinal separation clears them. This sets the packaging envelope. It does not establish electrical engagement of a particular received red pair. The public exchange contains two project-owned installation envelopes and the actual shop hardware. The restricted supplier STEP remains a private reproduction input. [Anderson red 992G1 and family CAD](https://www.andersonpower.com/product/sb50-standard-housings-red/) · [Anderson SB50 data sheet](https://www.andersonpower.com/content/dam/app/ecommerce/product-pdfs/DS-SB50.pdf)

Mount the fixed SB50 to the tray and structural deck at X−284, Y−34.30 and −15.25. From the 155 mm top datum fit Ø6/3.4 stepped spacers 1.889 and 1.127 mm long respectively. Both M3×25 socket heads seat at Z168.827 over 13.25 mm²; each uses an underside Ø7 washer and AF5.5×4 locking nut. The Ø3.4 through-bores pass through both sheets. The little lands on the actual housing underside need their two different spacer lengths. The mounted shell, screws, caps, seated nuts and both tools share one supplier comparison.

The bill retains the complete 1.65 kg base-harness allowance. Allocate up to 225 g of that same cap to both housings, four 1307 contacts, two finished battery leads, lugs and insulation, leaving at most 1.425 kg for all remaining base-harness articles. Both sides of this disconnect remain in the counted base-harness estimate. The offered am-4483 uses 6 AWG EPDM and 1/4 inch ring terminals. The NP18-12B has M5 through-blades. Measure actual crimp, contact and washer area, bolt grip, boot coverage, strain relief and harness weight before accepting this electrical assembly.
