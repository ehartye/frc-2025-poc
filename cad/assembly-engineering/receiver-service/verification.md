## Moving camera and cable carrier

**Hold the moving transition-board clamp for redesign.** The original camera-support drawing calls for an electrical insulator. The issued materials list assigns `support--insulating-board-clip` alloy steel and charges it as 1.115 g of steel at the upper density. The transition PCB itself is FR4. At the parked datum the aluminum support rod penetrates the clip by 6.000 mm³ and the PCB by 0.039 mm³. Both M2×4 screw envelopes end at the bottom of the clip without engaging a supporting metal thread. Moving the complete group with the slide preserves this conflict. Fabrication requires a specified dielectric, positive screw retention and a rod seat clear of the board. The current bill retains its steel charge. The preceding bill grouped 105 g of fixed camera adapter and leads with 170 g of factory moving data cable. The corrected bill adds the 220 g LAPP cut, 5 g moving Y adapter and 32.25 g power donor, bringing that group to 532.25 g. Three MEAN WELL DDR-30G-5 converters receive 130 g each against the manufacturer's 120 g packing entry. The other fixed-panel wiring, rail, protection and retention share 360 g of the same 750 g complete panel. The finished moving cable support already has 25 individual material rows.
