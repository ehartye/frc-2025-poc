## End switches and physical stops

Mount two Omron D2F-01L-D3 hinge-lever microload switches, one per physical end. Each is one bought switch including its blade. Omron gives approximately 0.5 g for the series. The purchasing budget allows 2 g each on receipt. Keep the independent SP1 slide sensor. The electrical hardware must distinguish sensor disagreement, disconnection and overtravel before any motion qualification. [Switch drawing](switch-mounts.step) · [Omron D2F family characteristics and dimensions](https://components.omron.com/us-en/system/files/2023-01/datasheet_pdf/B036-E1.pdf)

1. Machine the 6061 middle-stage cam, tongue and foot as one piece. The cam centre at stow is X−97.5, Y−150.5. Its lower plateau Z456.15 is 8 mm long, rising 4.5 mm over the adjoining 15 mm on either end. Secure its raised foot using two Accu SFE-M2-6-A2 slotted cheese-head screws at X−104 and −91, Y−167.50, into M2×0.4 tapped holes in the upper middle rail. Install this assembly before the outer moving pan. At stow the pan and its insert obstruct one of the two drivers. Gauge the 0.70 mm nominal lateral separation between the new foot edge and the adjacent fixed outer rail. Check rail straightness and screw-head clearance through the entire stroke.
2. Machine two fixed adjustment brackets with closed vertical slots on 6.50 mm pitch. Preassemble each switch using two fitted Ø1.90 mm shoulder pins through the switch and slot, 8.4 mm effective shoulder length, M2×0.4 threaded ends and AF4 locking nuts. Remove the corresponding physical stop for switch-side access; one wrong-sequence driver hits each stop. A 15 mm driver reaches both pins on the bench.
3. Attach the bare brackets to the bridge using four DIN 84 M2 slotted through screws, washers and AF4 locking nuts. Use the dimensioned axes in the STEP. Pocket-edge holes include Ø6.70 relief on the underside for full washer seating and tool access. Use a 6.5 mm OD socket below. Above, a 0.5 × 3.0 mm flat driver with a 3.10 mm shank requires fitting the retracted bracket before its two switch locknuts. The larger driver catches each nut by 0.059 mm³ if the switch is installed first. With the corresponding stops still off and the moving cam clear, insert the switch mounting pins laterally, set the switch and secure its nuts. Then fit the located hard stops. A drawing-sized straight driver on a bare bracket reaches all four base screws. Each head has 6.43 mm² of modeled seating. The full-diameter screw heads in the STEP are outer-envelope representations of the slotted head. 4. The two blocks are the travel limits if a fault bypasses the normally closed switches. Each includes a 3.8 × 4.0 mm tongue, 1.2 mm deep, in a matching 4.0 × 4.2 × 1.3 mm bridge pocket. One M3×8 screw comes from below the bridge into each block. Pressing the block 0.15 mm in X engages its retaining land after 0.10 mm clearance. Install the clamp screws before final travel setting. 5. With the drive isolated, set each switch to 0.35 mm additional tip travel after measured operation on its plateau. Hold residual setup error to ±0.10 mm. At this setting the switch has 0.25–0.45 mm overtravel against a catalog allowance of at least 0.55 mm. It asserts 9.67–11.00 mm of shelf travel before the corresponding endpoint and must release by 14.34 mm from that end on departure, including catalog differential. Confirm independent states while approaching and withdrawing from each end. The unadjusted low operating limit misses by 1.15 mm. The opposite catalog extreme would depress the same lever by 1.85 mm.
