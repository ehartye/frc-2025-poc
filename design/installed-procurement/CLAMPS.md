# Inner drive carrier and gearbox fastening

The selected shop-study candidate adds 32 positive clamp connections. Sixteen secure the four adjustable end carriers. Eight secure the two center carriers. Eight tie the adapter plates to the inner chassis rails. It replaces four original aluminum end carriers and two original aluminum gearbox faceplates. Its 86-body STEP includes every candidate plate, screw, washer and square block. The [545-body reference assembly](../competition-package/shop-duplex/README.md) retains its original geometry and registration.

## Assembly and service

1. Mount each gearbox to its new plate with the two original #10-32 screws and confirm actual thread depth. Fit the plate to the inner rail, align the common shaft and start all four rail screws. Fit each fixed center carrier and its four M5 screws. Torque and witness before closing the wheel bay. The 25 mm straight approach to some center screws crosses both center sprockets when assembled.
2. Seat one square nut in each end-carrier pocket with its broad face flat. Start inboard screws and 2 mm washers while the carrier is accessible, then fit bearings, shafts, wheels and chains. Captive flats prevent rotation. The square blocks can fall free with their screws fully removed, so support the assembly before disassembly. The slot geometry covers ±4 mm. Move each end axle, both bearings and both jack tips together.
3. For adjustment, loosen the paired clamps enough to move, set measured parallelism and chain tension, retighten, check free running and mark the heads. All 16 inboard screw approaches clear a 25 mm-long tool envelope at the registered position. Verify the complete handle, fingers, access at slot extremes, actual sprocket/chain and installed wiring on the first article.
4. Gauge and fixture the clamp before loading. At the prior 38 A drive limit, the single-loop case reaches 616 N tight plus 25 N slack. Conservatively assign the whole 641 N longitudinal resultant to one end carrier. A factor of 2 and assumed interface friction 0.15 require 2.14 kN preload per screw across four clamps. Friction 0.10 raises this to 3.21 kN. The 10 mm square block gives about 78 mm² nominal net face area, or 27.4 MPa bearing pressure at 2.14 kN. Actual flattened contact area, friction, steel thread strength, pocket-floor breakout, corner fatigue, other wheel reactions, shock and strain in the lightened plate require coupon and fixture tests. The calculation specifies no production torque. [Original drivetrain calculation](../drivetrain/README.md)

## Native and weight comparison

The four actual red STEP files from the registered 545-body source were reopened. The 86 proposed parts cleared one another and all 539 retained bodies at all four poses. End carriers cleared at −4, 0 and +4 mm on both sides when axle, bearing, spacers and take-up jack followed the carrier. The check also includes both fixed center stations, for 14 mounting cases. It verifies the sampled adjustments and each named solid on independent STEP reopen. It uses suppressed threads, modeled sprocket boundaries and rigid hardware. [Candidate fit and service evidence](clamp-review.json)

The initial alternative used 16 steel clamp screws threaded directly into four replacement steel end carriers and two full rectangular steel center plates. It passed its nominal fit checks at **+0.673205 kg**. A prior layout reached the end screws through the wheel bay, where the straight tool struck each end sprocket. The lighter version keeps the end screws accessible from inside while replacing the end carrier stock with aluminum and captive steel threads, at **+0.377621 kg**. The difference is 0.295584 kg. The pocket adds one machining operation family and 16 retained square pieces. Direct steel threads offer a simpler machining fallback if the robot can meet its weighed mass target. [Steel comparison](steel-review.json)

The previous upper R103 margin of 0.892163 kg becomes **0.514542 kg** with the lighter clamp set. Combining the separate 0.508 kg end-stop envelope reduces the theoretical margin to **about 6.5 g before its mounting bolts**, machining variation, omitted items and scale uncertainty. With the all-steel clamp set, the same combination exceeds the upper estimate by 0.289 kg. Treat simultaneous adoption as an open mass and structural decision.
