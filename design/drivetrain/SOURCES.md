A 16T driver and ordinary RS25 were considered. A smaller pitch radius increases strand tension at equal axle torque. The stronger 32T/BF25H choice retains the same 1:1 center-to-end transmission and leaves a calculated 140 N catalog-load margin per fully loaded loop.
