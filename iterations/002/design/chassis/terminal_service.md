# Bumper support in the four-corner chassis

The two Red 2 trips require the robot to dock under control, retain its bumper and remain serviceable. The 552-body Fusion issue includes all four corners and four bolted terminal cartridges. The ring, backing and complete upper remain proposed. The procedure below comes from the first 504-body issue, which installed the port cartridge.

For the port cartridge, insert the captive block into the open socket rail 14 mm below its final elevation, with the access-side swerve module removed. Slide it to the four anchor axes, raise its shoulders through the matched rail holes and fit the two side keepers. Install the compression spacers, terminal and load washers before tightening the four primary screws. The six modeled threads have cold-verified fits at their seated heads. Their CAD angles belong to this model's thread starts. A replacement screw must run freely into the tapped part and reach its actual head seat. Achieved preload still requires a procedure for the selected fastener finish and joint.

For removal, support the frame and unload the bumper. Remove the one access-side module with its rail screws, undo the cartridge fasteners, lower the captive block and withdraw it. At final height the block overlaps 1,026 mm³ of rail along the extraction path. Leaving the module screws installed also obstructs its lowered path. For the four lower primary screws, the provisional 6–9 kN clamp window cannot be guaranteed by torque alone across the trial friction range of 0.12–0.18 and ±4% applied torque. A 4–8 kN window is compatible with the calculated loads because the locating shoulders carry transverse shear. An illustrative 7.2 N·m dial setting gives 4.63–7.05 kN in that model. Even at the lower 4 kN bound, the 20% settling case retains 2.40 kN against the worst factored service uplift. Establish the actual torque–tension relation on the selected finish, nutbar and load washer before issuing a torque specification. Washer bending, keeper retention and the separately sized outer bumper fasteners have their own checks. [Torque, preload and signed load cases](port-preload.json)

The proposed positive containment must survive loss of any one primary backing attachment without the bumper falling, peeling outward into a wheel or dragging. A surrounding rail with low stress does not prove that condition. Each removable section requires at least two independently retained connections to the frame, separated in plan and capable of resisting rotation and vertical withdrawal. Use the actual combined wrench, including both signs of reversal, against every single attachment loss. Check its trapped position with the actual wheel sweep and other rule clearances. A connection that remains joined but permits a wheel strike is a failed containment case. The independent retaining hardware, actual backing and force envelope are still to be measured on a complete installed assembly.

Before play and after contact, compare exposed fastener witnesses and the backing datum, check for a missing keeper, separated seam, permanent set, torn hole, displaced padding or new wheel interference. On any change, cease driving when safe and return the robot for inspection. Restore the positive connection and verify both wheel clearance and backing registration before subsequent operation. Routine inspection does not qualify continued full-speed operation with a missing primary attachment.
